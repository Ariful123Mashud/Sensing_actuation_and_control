Simulations, code, and lab material for RAS ZG502 Sensing, Actuation and Control (BITS Pilani WILP): robot sensors, actuators, kinematics, dynamics, and control, with CoppeliaSim, Python, and MATLAB examples used in class.
# RAS ZG502: Sensing, Actuation and Control

This repository contains the simulations, code, and lab material discussed
in the contact sessions of RAS ZG502 (Sensing, Actuation and Control) at
BITS Pilani WILP. The course takes a robotics-oriented, application-first
view of how robots sense their environment, drive their actuators, and
close the loop with control.

## What's inside

- **Sensing:** characterisation of range sensors, IMUs, and cameras;
  perception sensors (GNSS, LiDAR, ToF, depth)
- **Actuation:** DC/servo/stepper motor models, drivers, and actuator sizing
- **Control:** PID design, state-space methods, and LQR
- **Simulation scenes:** CoppeliaSim scenes with accompanying scripts
